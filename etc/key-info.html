Paper key information

This personalizes your paper key: a letter to whoever may need it, the people who can help them, and any notes. Nothing leaves this page until you save, and then only to your Downloads folder, where valt picks it up.

The letter

The first page of the paper key is a short letter from you.

People who can help

If you are unavailable, whoever has the paper key may need help with the technical steps. Add at least one person with computer skills (the command line on macOS or Linux) who has agreed to help, with ways to reach them.

Notes

Anything else the reader should know, one item per line. If you will not write your passphrase on the paper key, say here where to find it.
